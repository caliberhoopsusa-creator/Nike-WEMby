03 — THE BRIEF

What I would build at Nike on day one.

A four-part system that operationalizes everything above. Built modularly — could be piloted with Wemby and replicated for every signature athlete on the Nike roster.

Cultural Signal Engine

An AI system that monitors social platforms, sports media, and fashion press for emerging cultural moments tied to a Nike athlete — surfacing them to the marketing team before they peak.

  • Input: Athlete handles, brand mentions, cultural keywords
  • Output: Daily ranked feed of activation opportunities
  • Win condition: Nike posts inside a moment, not after

Campaign Co-Pilot

The tool you just used, scaled. AI-generated creative briefs that take a product line and audience and produce 5–10 distinct campaign directions — each with headline, visual treatment, and channel mix.

  • Saves: ~40 hours of junior strategist time per campaign
  • Output: Editable brief, ready for creative review
  • Win condition: 10× the iteration speed at half the cost

Personalized Drop Targeting

Every Nike app member sees a different Wemby story. AI matches user behavior (workouts, browsing, region, age bracket) to one of dozens of pre-built narrative angles — same product, infinite framings.

  • Input: Nike app member graph + content library
  • Output: Per-user content variant at drop time
  • Win condition: Higher drop conversion, deeper retention

Sponsorship Fit Scoring

Built from my work on Caliber — an AI scouting platform I founded that analyzes basketball talent. Adapted for Nike: score every NCAA + EuroLeague prospect on cultural fit, brand alignment, and growth trajectory. Find the next Wemby before the bidding war.

  • Trained on: Athlete media, social, performance data
  • Output: Ranked sponsorship target list
  • Win condition: Lock the alien before the eclipse
