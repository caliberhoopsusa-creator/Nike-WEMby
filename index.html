    04 — The Brief

    What I'd build at Nike on day one.

    The four-part system behind everything above. Modular — pilot it with Wemby, replicate it for every signature athlete on the roster.

    Cultural Signal Engine

    The live panel above, productionized. Monitors social, sports media, and fashion press for emerging moments tied to a Nike athlete — surfacing them before they peak.

    • Input: Athlete handles, brand mentions, cultural keywords
    • Output: Daily ranked feed of activation opportunities
    • Win condition: Nike posts inside a moment, not after

    Campaign Co-Pilot

    The tool you just used, scaled. Takes a product line and audience and produces distinct campaign directions — each with headline, treatment, and channel mix.

    • Saves: ~40 hours of junior strategist time per campaign
    • Output: Editable brief, ready for creative review
    • Win condition: 10× iteration speed at half the cost

    Personalized Drop Targeting

    Every Nike app member sees a different Wemby story. AI matches behavior (workouts, browsing, region, age) to one of dozens of narrative angles — same product, infinite framings.

    • Input: Nike app member graph + content library
    • Output: Per-user content variant at drop time
    • Win condition: Higher drop conversion, deeper retention

    Sponsorship Fit Scoring

    Built from my work on Caliber — an AI scouting platform I founded. Adapted for Nike: score every NCAA + EuroLeague prospect on cultural fit, brand alignment, and trajectory. Find the next Wemby before the bidding war.

    • Trained on: Athlete media, social, performance data
    • Output: Ranked sponsorship target list
    • Win condition: Lock the alien before the eclipse
